# Southeast Asia Burned Area Dataset (`data/`)

This directory contains the raw observational/reanalysis datasets (`data/Raw_Data/`), the preprocessing script (`data/process_all_kaggle_data_025.py`), and the **0.25° × 0.25° Kaggle-ready training archives** covering Southeast Asia from **January 2003 to December 2020** (216 months).

---

## 1. Raw Data Sources (`data/Raw_Data/`)

### 1.1 GFED4s — Ground-Truth Burned Area (`Raw_Data/GFED4s/`)
Global Fire Emissions Database v4 with small fires (0.25° monthly NetCDF files):
- **Monthly grids (`BA199701.nc` – `BA202012.nc`)**: 288 monthly global files containing total burned area fraction and fire-type partitioning (`Peat`, `Defo` [deforestation], `Crop` [agricultural waste], `Sava`, `BorF`, `TemF`).
- **Regional aggregates (`gfed4s_se_asia.nc`, etc.)**: Pre-extracted regional subsets.
- Used both as the ground-truth target ($y$, burned area fraction) and to construct lagged deforestation/cropland fire memory features (`defo_ba_lag3`–`24`, `crop_ba_lag1`–`24`, `years_since_first_defo`, `defo_ba_cumulative_13_24mo`, and 9-/12-month cumulative sums using the 2001–2002 lookback buffer).

### 1.2 ERA5 & ERA5-Land Reanalysis (`Raw_Data/ERA5/` & `Raw_Data/ERA5_SEAsia/`)
ECMWF 5th Generation Atmospheric and Land Reanalysis (0.25° monthly files, 2000–2020), clipped to the Southeast Asia study domain in `ERA5_SEAsia/`:
- **Single-Level Surface (`era5_single_level_*_seasia.nc`)**: 2m temperature (`t2m`), 2m dewpoint (`d2m`), surface pressure (`sp`), boundary layer height (`blh`), and 10m wind components (`u10`, `v10`). Also used to derive Vapour Pressure Deficit (`era5_vpd`) and the 3-/6-month cumulative drought stress index (`drought_stress_kbdi_proxy_3mo/6mo`).
- **Pressure Levels (`era5_pressure_levels_*_seasia.nc`)**: Lower-tropospheric temperature and winds at 850 hPa (`t_850hPa`, `u_850hPa`, `v_850hPa`), plus mid-tropospheric vertical velocity and geopotential height at 500 hPa (`w_500hPa`, `z_500hPa`).
- **Total Precipitation (`era5_precip_*_seasia.nc`)**: Monthly precipitation (`tp`) at lags 0, 1, and 3 months.
- **Multi-Layer Soil (`era5_soil_*_seasia.nc`, `era5_soil_deep_*_seasia.nc`)**: Volumetric soil water and soil temperature across layer 1 (0–7 cm: `swvl1`, `stl1`), layer 2 (7–28 cm: `swvl2`, `stl2`), and layer 3 (28–100 cm: `swvl3`, `stl3`) at lags 0, 1, and 3 months, plus 6-month cumulative layer-3 soil moisture deficit (`era5_swvl3_deficit_6mo`).

### 1.3 MODIS Vegetation Indices (`Raw_Data/MODIS/`)
Terra MODIS Monthly 1 km Vegetation Indices (**MOD13A3 v061**):
- **Main Archive (`MOD13A3.061_1km_aid0001.nc`)**: ~7.1 GB NetCDF stack over Southeast Asia, accompanied by VI quality lookup tables (`*-VI-Quality-lookup.csv`) and QA statistics.
- Aggregated to the 0.25° grid to produce raw NDVI and EVI (`ndvi_raw`, `evi_raw`), leak-free standardized monthly anomalies (`ndvi_anom`, `evi_anom` + lags 1–12 mo), cumulative negative vegetation stress (`ndvi_cumdeficit_3mo/6mo/12mo`), month-over-month greenness change (`ndvi_anom_delta`), and drought–vegetation interaction terms (`ndvi_drought_interact`).

### 1.4 Static Soil Organic Carbon (`Raw_Data/carbon/`)
- **SoilGrids 250m (`organic.carbon.stock_msa.kgm2_m_250m_b0t10cm_..._v0.2.tif`)**: Topsoil (0–10 cm) organic carbon stock density in $\text{kg m}^{-2}$ (~1.37 GB GeoTIFF).
- Resampled to 0.25° (`static_soc`) alongside a peatland extent indicator (`peat_mask`).

## 2. Kaggle-Ready 0.25° Datasets (Generated by `python data/process_all_kaggle_data_025.py`)

All variables are aligned onto a unified **0.25° × 0.25° Southeast Asia grid** (`168 lat × 268 lon = 45,024 pixels`) over **216 months** (Jan 2003 – Dec 2020), totaling **9,725,184 spatiotemporal rows** with 0% NaNs (temporal gaps imputed via 2003–2011 pixel historical means; all-null boundary pixels imputed via nearest valid pixels within the same sub-region). All standardized anomalies are computed strictly from the **2003–2011 pre-test training period** (first 108 months).

### 2.1 Feature Matrix (`data/bbur_enhanced_training_ready_seasia_025.npz`)
- **Dimensions**: `x_flat` of shape `9,725,184 × 104` (`float32`), plus `y_flat`, `valid_mask`, `is_zero_flux`, `time_idx`, `lat_flat`, `lon_flat`, `feature_names` (`104,`), `years_pm` (`216,`), `lat` (`168,`), `lon` (`268,`).
- **104 Columns in `feature_names`**:
  - **Seasonality & Static Masks (5)**: `ann_sin`, `ann_cos`, `static_soc`, `peat_mask`, `fire_season`
  - **ENSO & Drought Proxies (7)**: `strong_enso`, `oni_lag0`–`oni_lag3`, `drought_stress_kbdi_proxy_3mo/6mo`
  - **ERA5 Surface, Pressure-Level & 3-Layer Soil (58)**: 13 surface/pressure-level variables × 3 lags (0, 1, 3), `era5_vpd` (lags 0, 1, 3), `era5_tp` (lags 0, 1, 3), deep soil layers `era5_swvl2/3` & `era5_stl2/3` × 3 lags (0, 1, 3), and `era5_swvl3_deficit_6mo`
  - **GFED4s Fire Memory & Land-Use History (19)**: `burned_area_pred`, `defo_ba` (lags 3, 6, 12, 18, 24), `years_since_first_defo`, `crop_ba` (lags 1, 2, 3, 6, 12, 18, 24), `defo_ba_cumulative_13_24mo`, and Mainland 9-/12-month cumulative sums (`defo_ba_cumulative_9mo/12mo`, `crop_ba_cumulative_9mo/12mo`)
  - **MODIS Vegetation Dynamics (14)**: `ndvi_raw`, `ndvi_anom` (lags 0, 1, 3, 6, 12 mo), `ndvi_cumdeficit_3mo/6mo/12mo`, `ndvi_anom_delta`, `ndvi_drought_interact`, `evi_raw`, `evi_anom` (lags 0, 1 mo)
  - **Legacy Column Excluded at Notebook Ingestion (1)**: `cams_tc_ch4` (retained for index compatibility, dropped via `BASE_EXCLUDE` in all 8 notebooks)

### 2.2 GFED4s Target Archive (`data/gfed4s_targets_seasia_025.npz`)
- **Contents**: Ground-truth observational burned area fractions aligned row-for-row (`9,725,184` rows) with `x_flat`:
  - `y_total_flat`: Total burned area fraction per 0.25° pixel
  - `y_peat_flat`, `y_defo_flat`, `y_crop_flat`: Partitioned burned area fractions by fire regime
  - `active_pixel_mask`, `time_idx`, `lat_flat`, `lon_flat`, `lat` (`168,`), `lon` (`268,`), `years` (`216,`)
- **Area Conversion**: Converting fractional burned area to physical area ($\text{km}^2$) uses cosine-latitude cell area:
  $$\text{Area (km}^2\text{)} = \text{fraction} \times (0.25 \times 111.32)^2 \times \cos(\text{lat})$$
